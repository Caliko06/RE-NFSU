## What every proposal shares (race_setup.md "A new race in UG2 space needs")
- Results table keeps 4 racers (3 rivals); >3 rivals in one race is unproven (tools/recomp/patches_race.py:90-107). UG2 and NFSU1 markers both give 8 grid slots, so slots are not the limit.
- A new route needs 0x34148/49 progress nodes and the 0x34146 start/finish markers in the loaded track. TRACKB1010 has the 42 km tour route and 20 markers (FACT, decoded here: 0x34148 flags 0x100, 853 nodes, 41,872.6 m). Whether a second route can be switched in per event at run time: UNKNOWN.
- Rivals walk the road graph, not the route (tour.md): at a link type 2 is always taken, 5/7 never, others by a random draw. The live UG2 forks are written 0/7 (tools/ug2_traffic.py docstring "types"), so a rival on a UG2 route may leave it; UG2's own race files re-tag these bytes and their meaning is not decoded. Chosen route = type 2 along it (the tour.py method) is the only proven way.
