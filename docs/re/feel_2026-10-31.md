# Feel and look complaints, RE pass (2026-10-31)

OWNER complaints after playing Remaster on :5180: blur too strong, lamp halos ("astigmatism", also when stopped),
camera low and flat at speed (car small), acceleration too fast (0-60 mph in ~3 s), garage cars look wrong.
Tools: REA (Ghidra provider) on pc/Speed.exe, census/hopper/decomp.c, our code (patches.py, host/, web/), the OWNER's
session JSONs (~/Downloads/nfsu-session-2026-10-31T14-56 and T15-18), screenshots and video of 2026-10-31.
V = read in code or bytes, I = inferred, U = open.

## Frame fact: "Original" mode is not the shipped game (V)
Every PATCH in tools/recomp/patches.py has `when=lambda: True`, so Original mode also runs the projection caves
(0x40debb), car cube 512 px (0x702a84), cube reflection x0.7 (0x707fc4), LOD lock (0x56e0d2). Host bridges flare size
(host/lights.c 0x6b633c) and rear grip 0.18 (host/rivals.c 0x6ffdd4) are Remaster only: Original sets RN_LIGHTS=0, ?handling=0.
The only shipped reference is the Wine exe (tools/wine/mkexe.py default: just the movie patch 0x4f5891).

## 1. Camera (V for code, numbers computed from it)
Speed.exe: horizontal FOV fixed at F = 71.5 deg (0x484b53, P[0] = 1/tan(F/2) in 0x40dd00). Chase rows per car
(dist -5.80, height 1.70 for the R34, logged) via 0x48b8c0. FOV row 0x6fbdf4 is constant per POV.
Ours, stacked in Remaster:
- Projection caves 0x40debb: vertical half-tangent = tan(F/2) / (512/448) -> 64.4 deg vertical, 96 deg horizontal at 16:9.
  A PC 4:3 view widened to 16:9 (Hor+) would be 56.7 deg vertical, 87.6 deg horizontal.
- ?speedfov=10 (host/d3d9.c fov_frame) writes 0x6fbdf4 up to 80.5 deg: 73 deg vertical, 105 deg horizontal.
- ?chasecam=1: distance x1.12, height x0.90 at speed. ?nitrocam=1: another x1.10 / x0.95 while nitrous burns.
Car size on screen ~ 1 / (tan(vfov/2) * distance). PC Hor+ at rest = 1.00. Remaster at rest 0.86, at full speed 0.65.
The camera is also 10 % lower. That is the "chasing my own car" the OWNER sees. None of it comes from Speed.exe.

## 2. Blur (V)
Mechanism 0x40cd40 is RE'd. Values: the OWNER's browser keeps preset 3 "PS2" (log: ramp 0.10-25.00 m/s, cap 0.260):
weight 0.24 at 9 mph and full 0.39 from 56 mph. Shipped PC (?blur=5): ramp 25-125 m/s, cap 0.65 -> 0 below 56 mph,
0.30 at 100 mph, 0.62 at 148 mph. So ours blurs city speeds the shipped game leaves sharp. Default for new browsers
is preset 6 (our depth-weighted PS2 imitation, web/gpu.js), also not shipped.

## 3. Lamp halos (V for code, I for the look)
- The star with rays is the game's own flare texture (Original-mode screenshot 9.18.41: star + round glow).
- Shipped light-streak buffer 0x409bdb: a still lamp builds to R 2.0x, G 2.4x, B 3.4x its flare (blue-white, blinding).
  Ours sets 1.5x on all channels ([0x707fcc]).
- Remaster adds bloom (web/gpu.js:357, BRIDGE, bright pass luma 0.72, 16 px gauss, weight 0.2), on unless ?bloom=0.
  docs/flare_re.md marks it NOT VERIFIED on screen. A soft wide glow when stopped comes from this bloom plus the 1.5x buffer.
- U: whether the halo is also wider than tall. Needs one 1:1 shot of a still lamp with ?bloom=0.

## 4. Garage cars (V)
Remaster runs host/sm1.c carlook 3 "live" (default when RN_CARLOOK is unset) and cardetail 1. carlook 3 reads the cube
as c*c*(1 + 4*lum^3): bright texels up to 5x. The garage ceiling lamps in the cube become white patches. Their edges follow
the polygons because the reflection vector comes from low-poly vertex normals. Same failure the OWNER called "white
blobs" on 2026-10-26. Original mode uses carlook 2003 (car.efx as shipped, but with the 0.35 cube scale and 512 px cube).

## 5. Acceleration (V for code, U for the answer)
- Physics data = BuildCarPhysicsInfo 0x575960(stock CarTypeInfo+0x110, spec = RideInfo+8 installed levels), called from
  RebuildPhysicsInfo 0x57f2b0. Torque 0x46cff0 = curve(rpm) * Engine+0xa0 * throttle.
- The unlock byte 0x735ec1 does not install parts: its readers are front-end functions only (0x4aa0f0..0x5a2db0;
  none in 0x46xxxx-0x47xxxx or 0x575960-0x58xxxx). It marks parts as bought and unlocked.
- Time is right: race clock = wall clock (video: 10.08 s of race timer in 10 s), 60 frames per 1.00 s, limiter 0x585b94 1:1.
- OWNER session T15-18: 0 -> 9.9 -> 20.0 -> 25.4 -> 30.5 m/s at one-second steps: 0-60 mph in ~2.7 s.
- U: the installed levels of the OWNER's R34 (nothing logs RideInfo+8), and the shipped 0-60 for the same car.
  Only the Wine reference can say if 2.7 s is NFSU itself.
