# Prior art in the wild: making the launcher look like NFS Underground 2003

Tags: FACT = read at the source or measured. SNIPPET = seen only in a search result, page not opened.
INFERENCE = my reading or a starting value, not tested. UNKNOWN = searched, not found.
Budget: 20 searches in total (12 + 8). No game runs, no Chrome, no builds. Local frames were only resized and measured.
No EA, vendor manual, MDN or Wikipedia source is used for technique. Font licences do cite the licence files.

## Read this first
- FACT: no one online publishes the NFSU1 frontend palette or font name, so the palette is measured from our own frames.
- FACT (re-nfsu-aa's measure): real game frames mean saturation 0.13, luma p99 117. Launcher 0.43 and 186.
- FACT: my own single frames agree on saturation order: race frame 0.29, launcher 0.51. Different frames, so use re-nfsu-aa's numbers as the target.
- FACT: the real menu is nearly one colour, dark teal, with white or pale type and no glow (`build/menu169.png`).
- FACT: the real dirt is in the splash art: scratches and scuffs over motion streaks (`build/fe_native.png`).
- INFERENCE: torn buttons are the OWNER's taste, not period accuracy. Real panels are clean cut polygons. A worn real-teal panel serves both.
- INFERENCE: the game's city has palm trees and US-style towers (`build/night/menu_world.png`). "Japanese" must come from cars, signs and type.

## 2. What modders actually set (NFSU2 ReShade/SweetFX, FACT from the preset pages)
- sherrydason, "Underground 2 Remastered": https://sfx.thelazy.net/games/preset/8690/
  - `fBloomSaturation=.46`, `fBloomThreshold=1`, `fBloomAmount=.8`, `fBloomTint=.83,.80,1`, `iBloomMixmode=3`.
  - `Levels BlackPoint=15`, `Curves Contrast=.35`, `Vibrance=.26`.
  - `FilmGrain2 grainamount=.05, coloramount=.6, lumamount=1, grainsize=1.6`.
  - Caveat: the page dumps every effect's values, so I cannot tell which sections were on.
- Dennis98, "Gamma-Fix/Better Lightning": https://sfx.thelazy.net/games/preset/2555/
  - Enabled: `Curves_contrast .17`, `Vibrance .10`, Tonemap `Gamma 1.585`, `Exposure .048`, `Saturation .07`. Bloom is off.
- Zomgerd, MasterEffect: https://sfx.thelazy.net/games/preset/3054/
  - Only FXAA, SSAO, LiftGammaGain and Reinhard are active. Bloom and grain are off.
- A_Person7778: AO, optional bloom, SMAA, sharpen, colour meant to look "more realistic", no bloom numbers: https://nfsmods.xyz/mod/5569
- Chavolatra: SMART_Sharp, Prod80_Bloom, qUINT_lightroom, qUINT_mxao, values not readable: https://sfx.k.thelazy.net/games/preset/9800/
- UNKNOWN: an NFSU1 preset with values. The Nexus NFSU mod cited in `scene.js:130` returned 403, so that claim is unverified.
- INFERENCE: of the three presets with readable values, two have bloom off. The one with bloom keeps it at .46 saturation, so glow goes pale, not coloured.
- INFERENCE: these modders mostly chase contrast and punch. Copy black point, pale bloom and grain, not their vibrance.

## 4. PS2-look repos and shaders
- FACT: bloom on PS2: threshold via subtractive blend, downsample, upsample, add back. Exposure used per-channel tables: https://xania.org/201003/swat-ps2-renderer
- FACT: a ReShade request sums it as "Threshold + Gaussian Blur + mix and then blend", no numbers: https://reshade.me/forum/shader-suggestions/5906-ps2-era-bloom?rCH=2
- FACT, MIT: threejs-psx-shader: about 320x240 buffer, 32 colour steps per channel, 4x4 ordered dither, passes for pixelation, dither, fog, CRT: https://github.com/lferreira457/threejs-psx-shader. PS1, not PS2.
- SNIPPET: Godot asset, 15-bit colour plus dither as one screen effect: https://store.godotengine.org/asset/fcidade/psx-godot-shader/
- SNIPPET: SkyGFX fork PR: 32 levels with 8x8 blue noise, bloom in one final pass: https://github.com/world69/skygfx/pull/1. FACT: 404 when opened.
- SNIPPET: cheap bloom for weak GPUs: https://github.com/hrydgard/ppsspp/blob/master/assets/shaders/bloom.fsh
- FACT: dither formula: add a Bayer 4x4 threshold, then `floor(c*(n-1)+.5)/(n-1)`: https://blog.maximeheckel.com/posts/the-art-of-dithering-and-retro-shading-web/
- UNKNOWN: interlace and PS2 frame ghosting. SkyGFX's README has no numbers, and its forum thread returned 403: https://github.com/aap/skygfx

Sodium light and wet road:
- FACT: Tanner Helland's curve gives 2000 K #FF890E, 2200 K #FF9227, 2500 K #FF9F46 (computed): https://tannerhelland.com/2012/09/18/convert-temperature-rgb-algorithm-code.html
- SNIPPET: photographers say sodium light cannot be white-balanced: https://www.talkphotography.co.uk/threads/sodium-lights-what-white-balance.118517/
- FACT: "Wet surface are diffusively darker and specularly brighter", with long stretched highlights that dim on rougher ground: https://seblagarde.wordpress.com/2012/12/10/observe-rainy-world/
- FACT: cheap fake: "blurry, stretched streaks of the bright lights below them", darker diffuse: https://github.com/VitoMinheere/RustChaos/issues/522

Applying it in our scene (FACT, local code):
- `web/launcher_b/scene.js:135-136`: `dehaze .25, vibrance .25, contrast 1.1, saturation 1.08`. `:138` fringing 2. `:162` final colour x1.25.
- Vendored PlayCanvas 2.22 bloom only takes `intensity` and `blurLevel` (`playcanvas.mjs:115876-115879`). Compose chunks `composeDeclarationsPS` and `composeMainEndPS` exist (`:111771-111805`).
- INFERENCE, untested: saturation .5, vibrance 0, dehaze 0, fringing 0, final gain 1.0, then measure toward 0.13 and p99 117.
- INFERENCE, untested: grain about .05 mostly luma, size 1.6, black point 15/255, in `composeMainEndPS`.

Torn edges, FACT, MIT, TornPaper v0.0.3 source: https://github.com/happy358/TornPaper
```html
<filter id="torn"><feTurbulence type="fractalNoise" baseFrequency=".03" result="paper_noise" numOctaves="10"/>
<feDiffuseLighting in="paper_noise" lighting-color="white" surfaceScale="3" result="paper"><feDistantLight azimuth="45" elevation="60"/></feDiffuseLighting>
<feTurbulence baseFrequency=".05" type="turbulence" numOctaves="10" result="edge_noise"/>
<feGaussianBlur stdDeviation=".5" in="SourceGraphic"/><feMorphology operator="erode" radius="5"/><feOffset dx="-2" dy="-2"/>
<feDisplacementMap scale="10" xChannelSelector="B" yChannelSelector="G" in2="edge_noise" result="edge"/>
<feComposite in="paper" in2="edge" operator="atop" result="r"/><feComposite in="SourceGraphic" in2="edge" operator="atop" result="s"/>
<feBlend mode="multiply" in="r" in2="s"/></filter>
```
- FACT: put turbulence on a pseudo-element, or it distorts the content: https://www.sitepoint.com/community/t/rough-edges-css/447119
- FACT: displaced edges come out jagged, fixed by a slight blur plus contrast. "SVG Filters can hurt the performance of your site drastically": https://www.smashingmagazine.com/2021/09/deep-dive-wonderful-world-svg-displacement-filtering/
- FACT: grain tile: `fractalNoise`, `baseFrequency .65`, `numOctaves 3`, `stitchTiles`, CSS `contrast(170%) brightness(1000%)`: https://css-tricks.com/grainy-gradients/
- SNIPPET: irregular `clip-path` torn strips on pseudo-elements: https://codepen.io/neptoo/pen/qBRPdgg
- UNKNOWN: a Y2K-grunge CSS kit or misregistered-print pen. Two offset text layers would be my idea.
- INFERENCE: bake the filter to one PNG or WebP per button shape, then `mask-image` it, so no live filter runs over the 3D scene.
